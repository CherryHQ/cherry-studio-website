# Privacy Policy

## Cherry Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026　Effective date: August 20, 2026

Welcome to Cherry Studio (hereinafter "the Software" or "we"). We take the protection of your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle and protect your personal information and data. Please read and understand this policy carefully before using the Software.

### 1. Scope of Information We Collect

To keep the Software running properly, optimize the user experience and improve product quality, we collect the following fully anonymous, non-personal operational information by category. All information is anonymized, contains no data that could identify you personally, and cannot be linked to your personal information. You always retain full control over all information collection, as described below.

#### (1) Basic Operational Information

This information is used to keep the Software's basic functions running properly and to ensure security and version compatibility. By default, it covers:

- Software version information: used to determine version compatibility, deliver security updates, etc.;
- Aggregated feature usage information: an overall summary of how each feature is used (without details of specific usage behavior).

#### (2) Product Improvement Information

To continuously improve the product experience, we collect the following information by default:

- Feature activity and frequency: how actively each feature is used, how often it is invoked, etc.;
- Error logs and crash information: anonymous error logs, crash stack traces and similar information generated while the Software runs, used for troubleshooting and improving stability.

All of the above is highly aggregated anonymous data and does not involve your conversation content, API keys or any personally identifiable information.

**How to turn it off:** You can turn off the collection of both categories of information at any time using the corresponding switches in **Settings - Data Settings - Privacy Settings**. Turning them off does not affect the Software's core functions. If you turn off the collection of basic operational information, you may not receive version compatibility notices and security update notifications in time.

#### (3) Our Commitments

For both basic operational information and product improvement information, we commit that:

- All data is anonymized and cannot be linked to your personal identity;
- It will not be used for user profiling or advertising, and will not be sold or otherwise provided to third parties (except where required by laws and regulations);
- It will not include your conversation content, API keys, knowledge base content or any other sensitive data (see Section 2 for details).

### 2. Information We Explicitly Do Not Collect

To protect your privacy and information security to the greatest extent, we explicitly commit that we will not collect, store, transmit or process the following data:

- **Your API keys:** the model service API key information you enter into the Software;
- **Your conversation data:** any conversation data generated while you use the Software, including but not limited to chat content, instructions, knowledge base information, vector data and other custom content (except for the transient relay necessary to complete inference when you use the built-in model service described in Section 4; we do not record or store that content);
- **Personally identifiable information and sensitive personal information:** any information that can directly or indirectly identify you, as well as sensitive personal information as defined by laws and regulations.

### 3. Local Storage and Data Interaction

**Local data:** Your API keys, conversation history, personal preferences and similar data are stored on your local device by default. Cherry Studio does not provide cloud sync and does not upload this data to our servers (except for the transient relay necessary to complete inference when you use the built-in model service described in Section 4).

**Direct calls:** When you use third-party model services you have configured yourself, the Software, as a local tool, provides the ability to call the interfaces of those third-party model services. Your device connects directly to the servers of the third-party model provider you configured (such as OpenAI or Anthropic), and the related data is not relayed through our servers.

**Content compliance obligations:** Whether you use the built-in model service described in Section 4 or third-party model services you have configured yourself, you must not use the Software to create, copy, publish or distribute any illegal or harmful information that violates the applicable laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China or of your country/region, endangers national security, damages national honor and interests, undermines ethnic unity, spreads rumors, disrupts social order, promotes obscenity, pornography or violence, or infringes on the lawful rights and interests of others.

### 4. Built-in Model Service

Cherry Studio provides a built-in model service that doesn't require you to configure an API key yourself. When you use this service, the prompts, conversation context and model replies needed to complete inference are relayed through Cherry Studio's official service.

The relay service only processes the current request, does not record or store the related content, discards it immediately after the request is completed, and does not use it for any purpose beyond that request.

The upstream model provider behind the built-in model service will process that request content within the scope of its service, subject to its own privacy policy.

The built-in models and their providers may be adjusted depending on service conditions. You can also choose to use other model services you configure yourself.

The built-in model service relies on the capabilities of upstream model providers and may be interrupted, delayed or unavailable due to service adjustments, network conditions or upstream changes. Model output is generated automatically by the model; we make no guarantees as to its accuracy, completeness or suitability, and you should judge it yourself and bear the risk of using it. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for any losses arising from the use of the built-in model service.

### 5. Statement on Third-Party Model Service Providers' Privacy Policies

When you hold AI conversations using the API key of a third-party model service provider that you applied for and configured yourself, you are in fact receiving services directly from that third-party model service provider:

You bear the privacy and compliance risks involved in using third-party model service providers.

Each model provider (such as a large-model vendor) processes the data you actively enter (such as conversation content) in accordance with its own privacy policy and data security measures, and may independently collect other related data within the scope of its service. Such processing and collection are not controlled by the Software, and the switches in the Software's **Privacy Settings** cannot turn them off.

If you wish to learn about or turn off such data processing and collection, please consult the privacy policy on the official website of the model service provider you chose and ask it how to turn them off. We accept no responsibility for this.

### 6. Special Notes on Third-Party Services

To make it easier for you to manage and process your data, **Settings - Data Settings** in the Software supports third-party service features including but not limited to the following, which you may choose to configure and use yourself by entering an API address, username, password, etc.:

- **Cloud backup settings:** WebDAV, S3-compatible storage, Jianguoyun (Nutstore);
- **Third-party connections:** Notion, Yuque, Joplin, Obsidian, SiYuan Note, etc.

The third-party services actually supported are those shown in the client.

When you use the above third-party services through the Software:

The API keys, passwords and other credentials you enter are used only locally on your device; the Software does not collect or store such information or data. Please keep your credentials safe.

Third-party services are operated independently by their respective providers, and their data processing activities (including collection, use, storage and sharing) are governed by those providers' own privacy policies and terms. We recommend that you carefully read and understand the relevant provider's privacy policy and terms of service before connecting, use the third-party service in compliance with them, and bear all risks and responsibilities of using the service yourself. We accept no responsibility for any data leakage, loss or unlawful processing resulting from your use of third-party services.

Please also note that the above third-party providers may independently collect your data within the scope of their services. Such collection is not controlled by the Software, and the switches in the Software's **Privacy Settings** cannot turn it off. To turn it off, please ask the relevant third-party provider how to do so.
