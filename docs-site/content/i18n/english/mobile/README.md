# Cherry Studio Mobile

Cherry Studio Mobile is an AI client designed for Android, iPhone, and iPad. Connect your own model services to chat, configure agents, and generate images from a mobile device.

## Current status

The mobile app is currently in **beta**:

* Android is distributed as an official APK.
* iPhone and iPad builds are distributed through Apple TestFlight.
* Screens and capabilities may change quickly. Follow the behavior of the version installed on your device.

Open the [mobile option on the download page](https://cherryai.com/download?platform=mobile), or read the [installation guide](installation.md) first.

## What you can do

* Add built-in or custom model providers and organize the models you use.
* Switch models in a conversation and send text, images, or files.
* Create agents with their own instructions, model, and supported system tools.
* Generate and preview images with a configured image model.
* Use a touch-first interface across Android phones, iPhone, and iPad.
